About the Lecturer Call

The Mathematical Epidemiology programme prepares students to use mathematics, statistics, computing, and public-health reasoning to understand infectious disease dynamics, surveillance data, intervention design, and health decision-making in African contexts.

Courses are delivered in intensive teaching blocks. Each selected lecturer is expected to be in residence with the host centre for the full three-week block attached to their course, supporting lectures, practical sessions, assessment, student consultation, and coordination with the academic team.

This form collects lecturer identity, academic and professional background, preferred MathEpi host pathway, course preference, block-linked availability, proposed teaching plan, passport biodata page, and final confirmations for review by the programme team.

1
Block-based teaching

Applicants choose a course and confirm availability for the exact three-week block in which that course is scheduled.

2
In-residence commitment

By applying, lecturers acknowledge that selected courses require physical residence at the assigned host pathway for the full block period.

3
Focused application

The form captures the evidence reviewers need through structured questions, without asking for a CV upload at this stage.

Required documents

  • Only the passport biodata page needs to be attached. Nothing else is required at this stage.
