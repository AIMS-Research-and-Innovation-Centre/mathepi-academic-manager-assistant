The opportunity

The Mathematical Epidemiology Programme invites outstanding recent PhD graduates to join MathEpi as Tutorial Fellows, an Early Career Academic Fellowship for full-time members of the academic community.

The fellowship is designed as an early-career pathway towards careers in academia, research institutes, public health agencies, government, and international organisations. Fellows support a pan-African cohort of excellent students while developing their own research profile through a protected 70:30 model.

Fellows work alongside international lecturers, supervisors, fellow tutors, and the Academic and Research Manager in a residential academic community, with occasional evening and weekend commitments during intensive teaching periods.

1
Programme role

Fellows provide tutorials, hands-on academic support, marking, student consultations, and teaching-continuity support if a lecturer is unavailable.

2
Protected development time

About 70% of the appointment is dedicated to tutoring and student support, while about 30% supports productive research and career development in a collaborative work environment.

3
Residential appointment

Applicants should be ready for a full-time residential appointment at the KEMRI location in Nairobi, Kenya, with availability during intensive programme periods.

Who should apply

This call is for recent PhD graduates whose doctorate will have been completed within two years of the fellowship start date.

Applicants should hold a PhD in mathematics, statistics, mathematical epidemiology, applied mathematics, computational biology, biostatistics, public-health modelling, data science, artificial intelligence and machine learning, computational physics, scientific computing, operations research, or a closely related quantitative discipline.

Applicants should also show a strong record of applying mathematical and computational methods to real scientific or public-health problems.

How to apply

Complete the online application form by 19 July 2026 with personal and professional details, qualifications, doctoral research, teaching and mentoring experience, a brief tutoring philosophy, and a research and career-development plan.

  • Passport biodata page.
  • PhD certificate as a legible PDF showing the exact graduation month/date.
  • At least two structured references with title, name, affiliation, designation, relationship, email, and phone number.

Equality, diversity and inclusion: AIMS builds an inclusive community reflecting Africa's diversity. Applications from qualified candidates of all nationalities, genders and backgrounds, and from underrepresented groups, are strongly encouraged. Appointments are made solely on merit.
